Create a new Notion page recording the decisions made and the findings worth keeping from this session.

## Steps

1. Keep it to what stays useful later — decisions with the reasoning behind them, and findings that would cost time to rediscover. Leave out the play-by-play of the work.
2. Title the page after what was decided, not after what the session was about.
